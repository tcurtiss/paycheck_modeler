Paycheck Modeling Calculator

A standalone, in-browser model of a full-year paycheck: 401(k) (regular + catch-up + after-tax), employer match, NQDC, HSA, ESPP, RSU vesting, NQDC payouts, cash bonuses, and federal/FICA/state withholding, all computed client-side. Nothing here is sent anywhere.

Compensation

Basics
Biweekly pay (26 checks a year) is the only cadence this model supports. Each check is dated 14 days after the last.
Cash bonuses

Each bonus is its own off-cycle check on its pay date. The elections below apply to every bonus.

Most employers withhold a separately paid bonus at the flat supplemental rate (22%; set under Tax Year Settings). Aggregate annualizes the bonus as if you were paid that much every period, which withholds much less on a small bonus and more on a large one.
The 401(k) and after-tax percentages apply to the bonus after any NQDC deferral.

NQDC Payouts (Distributions)

One row per distribution election: a fixed amount paid on the same date each year for the given number of years, starting with the first payment date. Only payments that land in this model's year (the year of the first pay date) count. Withheld as federal supplemental wages at the flat rate (sharing the $1M cumulative threshold with bonuses and RSU vests), plus state. No Social Security/Medicare, since FICA was already applied when the money was deferred.

    At a Glance

    Where Each Paycheck Goes

    Each check's gross pay, split into taxes, retirement savings, NQDC deferral, and ESPP/other deductions, with take-home pay (hatched) on top. Includes off-cycle bonus checks and any NQDC payouts. Hover, or tab in and use arrow keys, for exact amounts; click or press Enter to jump to that row below.

    Cumulative 401(k) & HSA Through the Year

    How each 401(k) source (Traditional, Roth, catch-up Traditional & Roth, after-tax, and employer match) plus HSA contributions accumulate across the year. Any employer/wellness HSA contribution is modeled as landing with the first paycheck. A hatched segment means that source has hit its annual limit as of that paycheck.
