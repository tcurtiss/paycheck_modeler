Cumulative 401(k) & HSA Through the Year

How each 401(k) source — Traditional, Roth, catch-up (Traditional & Roth), after-tax, and employer match — plus HSA contributions stack up and accumulate across the year. Any employer/wellness HSA contribution is modeled as landing with the first paycheck. A hashed segment means that source has hit its annual max as of that paycheck.

Runs entirely in your browser — inputs are only saved to this browser's local storage, never sent anywhere. Not tax advice; verify against your actual pay stubs and a tax professional for anything consequential.
